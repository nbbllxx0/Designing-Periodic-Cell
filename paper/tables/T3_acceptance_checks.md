- Every check is acceptance under the stated discretization, sampling and guard assumptions, not a continuum theorem. Values are read from the checker source with `ast`/regex at generation time; 'Source' gives file:line at that time (file hashes in the ledger).
- Verdicts: certified (guarded bound clears every target), refuted (the opposite guarded bound misses it, or the geometry is not a connected load-bearing solid), undetermined (target inside the guard band, not converged, or a solver failure). Only certified releases a design.
